# Affinity And First Gift

The first Gift is discovered through player behavior instead of selected from a
free list.

During the early game and tutorial phase, the system tracks hidden affinity
signals. These signals accumulate affinity tokens for Gift schools.

## Affinity Sources

Affinity can come from:

- combat style;
- defensive or aggressive choices;
- mobility, stealth, or kiting behavior;
- use of traps or clever positioning;
- rift types closed by the player;
- dialogue and quest decisions;
- support, reward-seeking, risk-taking, or protective behavior.

Examples:

- aggressive pressure can increase Fire affinity;
- tanking and endurance can increase Earth or Light affinity;
- mobility and evasion can increase Air affinity;
- shadowy or selfish choices can increase Darkness affinity;
- clever manipulation can increase Arcane or Nature affinity.

These mappings are design examples, not final balancing rules.

## First Gift Selection

At Symbiote level 2, the game should run the first true awakening quest. The
player gives the Symbiote a name, learns that the previous "system" reactions
were connected to a bound entity, and then receives access to the first
conscious Gift choice.

At this first awakening, the game presents the player's top affinity results
instead of every possible school.

The intended shape:

1. The system evaluates hidden affinity tokens.
2. The naming quest reveals the Symbiote as more than system messages.
3. The top three schools or Gift candidates are selected.
4. The player chooses the first Gift from those options.

This should make the first Gift feel like a consequence of the player's actual
playstyle while still preserving player choice.

The first Gift should feel like the first stable magical form of the energy the
player has been collecting from rifts. It is not only a menu reward; it is the
moment where rift energy, hidden affinity, and the named Symbiote become a
usable construct.

## Replacement Later

The first Gift should not permanently lock the character.

Later systems should allow Gift replacement, recombination, or build
restructuring. The exact cost, timing, and restrictions are not defined yet.
