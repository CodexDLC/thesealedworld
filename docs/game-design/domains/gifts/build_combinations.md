# Build Combinations

Gifts are intended to combine into builds rather than act as isolated spells.

## Multiple Gifts

A character can eventually work with multiple Gifts. These Gifts may be from one
school or from different schools, depending on future slot, rank, and conflict
rules.

Combining Gifts can create:

- specialized mono-school builds;
- hybrid school builds;
- physical enhancement builds;
- defensive or sustain builds;
- burst resource builds;
- control or tempo builds.

## Composite Gifts

Some Gifts can evolve or combine into broader expressions.

Examples:

- stone plus metal can become a broader Earth expression;
- stone plus fire can create a magma expression;
- water plus darkness can support poison, drain, or attrition concepts;
- air plus light can support speed, focus, or radiant movement concepts.

These are design examples only. They should become concrete Gifts only after the
ability catalog, conflict rules, and balancing goals are defined.

## Conflicts

Gift combinations may have conflicts.

Conflict types can include:

- mutually exclusive schools;
- unstable combinations;
- rank-gated combinations;
- slot limitations;
- resource inefficiency;
- passive effects that cannot stack;
- identity conflicts where one Gift overrides another.

Conflict rules are important for build identity, but they should not be added as
hard mechanics until concrete Gift sets exist.

## Ability Shape

A mature Gift can expose:

- active abilities;
- passive abilities;
- combat triggers;
- modifier packages;
- resource generation changes;
- defensive or offensive rank scaling.

Implementation should eventually translate this design into ability definitions,
passives, combat catalog entries, modifiers, runtime state, and UI surfaces.
