# Schools And Gifts

The Gift system has eight core energy schools.

To the player, these schools can read as familiar fantasy magic. Internally,
they are energy directions: ways the Symbiote shapes accumulated rift energy
into effects the world can express.

| Key | School | Direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `fire` | Fire | Aggression, heat, explosions, burning pressure. |
| `water` | Water | Flow, poison, healing, adaptation, pressure control. |
| `air` | Air | Speed, lightning, movement, evasion, invisibility. |
| `earth` | Earth | Stone, metal, protection, weight, physical force. |
| `light` | Light | Barriers, cleansing, focus, protection, radiant force. |
| `darkness` | Darkness | Shadow, weakening, vampirism, concealment, attrition. |
| `arcane` | Arcane | Pure energy, reality pressure, unstable manipulation. |
| `nature` | Nature | Life, growth, beasts, thorns, regeneration, terrain. |

## Gifts Inside Schools

Each school can contain multiple Gifts. A school is a family, not a single
ability.

A Gift is a stable energy construct inside that family. It is not only a spell
button. It can contain active releases, passive contours, reactions, body
enhancement, weapon enhancement, protection, control, or resource behavior.

For example, Earth can contain a stone Gift, a metal Gift, a weight Gift, or a
broader Earth Gift that combines earlier expressions. Fire can contain direct
burning pressure, explosive force, heat weapon behavior, or volatile burst
effects.

One Gift should eventually provide a small kit, roughly:

- active abilities;
- passive abilities;
- possible triggers or reactive effects;
- combat modifiers;
- optional build constraints or conflicts.

A rough target is 5-6 ability-level effects per mature Gift, but this is not a
hard implementation rule yet.

## Non-Magical Expressions

Gifts can look like spells, but they do not have to.

A Gift can:

- increase weapon impact;
- add elemental or energy typing;
- improve armor behavior;
- increase resistance or recovery;
- alter token generation or spending;
- change tempo in combat;
- add passive protection;
- unlock a special action.

This keeps the Gift branch useful for physical builds, defensive builds, and
hybrid builds, not only caster-style play.
