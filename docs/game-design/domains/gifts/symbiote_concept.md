# Symbiote Concept

The Symbiote is the player's energy companion and the primary interface to rift
power. It is not a classic spellbook, class system, or external magic source.
It is a living or semi-living energy system bound to the character.

Before the first awakening, the player does not know the Symbiote as a separate
entity. The player sees system messages, progress signals, strange reactions,
and rift-energy feedback, but the source is still hidden behind the interface.

The first real relationship begins when the Symbiote reaches level 2. At this
point the game should use a story quest where the player gives the Symbiote a
name. Naming is the moment when the system mask cracks: the hidden mechanism
becomes a bound presence, and the first Gift can become conscious control
instead of unexplained feedback.

## Role

The Symbiote owns the energy branch of progression:

- it interprets the player's affinity profile;
- it enables Gifts;
- it increases the effectiveness of Gift abilities;
- it can enhance both offensive and defensive combat capabilities;
- it connects the player to rift energy and special resources.

The Symbiote can support magical-looking abilities, but the design should avoid
treating Gifts as generic fantasy magic at the system-design level. For the
player, Gifts can look almost like classic magic: fire, light, darkness,
barriers, poison, lightning, healing, roots, curses, or body enhancement.

In world logic, this "magic" is controlled release of accumulated rift energy
through the Symbiote. A Gift is the form that release takes. It may create fire,
harden the body, improve weapon impact, reinforce defenses, increase recovery,
distort tempo, or change how a combat exchange resolves.

## Relationship To Skills

Skills and Gifts are separate progression branches.

Skills are trained through use and represent mastery. They improve because the
character practices weapons, armor, tactics, crafting, survival, or social
actions.

Gifts grow through the energy path. Their progression is tied to rift closure,
special resources, Symbiote growth, and future energy events.

The two branches can interact in combat. For example, a Gift may strengthen a
sword strike, convert part of an attack into elemental damage, improve defense,
or add a passive effect to an existing skill-driven action.

## Design Status

This is a concept document. It defines direction and terminology, but it does
not define DTOs, database schema, frontend UI contracts, or final formulas.
