The Fragmented Memory

Codex Entry: The Unseen Guest

The survivors of the first Rifts speak of a "cold weight" that settled in their blood during the transition. They returned seeing things that shouldn't exist—pulsing ley-lines in the air, numbers that dictate fate, and a ghostly overlay that masks the ruins.

No one knows why this "vision" exists. Some call it a curse from the Between, others a blessing of a new era. All we know is that as you grow stronger, the whispers in the back of your mind grow louder, and the Vision becomes more real than the world itself.
